WT41N0 USB Flasher

Sends a Symbol HEX image to a Zebra / Symbol WT41N0 in download mode, like support/flash.py. Needs a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge) and this page opened from localhost or HTTPS.

This browser has no WebUSB support.
Use Chrome or Edge, and open the page through http://localhost (for example python3 -m http.server in this folder) or HTTPS.

1Choose the image

2Put the unit in download mode

  1. Hold P1 or P2 while switching the unit on.
  2. Choose Download from USB and connect the USB cable or cradle.

3Flash

 
Keep the cable connected until the transfer finishes. The unit then stores the image; it does not restart by itself, so power-cycle it afterwards.
